What's inside

Everything starts with a safe place to talk.

Then it goes deeper — at your pace.

Talk it through, any hour

A supportive AI companion that's there at 3pm or 3am, with coping strategies and gentle reflection prompts — not just a chatbot script.

Know where you stand

Short, plain-language check-ins for anxiety, depression, and stress — with results you can actually understand, not clinical jargon.

Write it down, safely

A private daily journal with mood tags and voice-to-text. Over time, MindCare gently surfaces patterns you might not notice yourself.

Watch your patterns

A calm dashboard of mood trends, a personal wellness score, and self-care tracking — so progress feels visible, not abstract.

Never alone in a hard moment

If signs of real distress show up, MindCare says so gently — and points you toward trusted people and crisis resources, every time.

Bring in a human, when ready

Connect with volunteer mentors or request an appointment with a counselor — on your terms, with easy follow-ups.

How it works

No app should ask more of you than you have to give.

First

Share, in your own words

Open the companion or a quick check-in. Type, talk, or just sit with the breathing orb. There's no wrong way to start.

Then

Reflect, with gentle structure

MindCare reflects patterns back to you in your diary and dashboard — never a diagnosis, always a starting point for self-understanding.

Over time

Grow, at your own pace

Build small habits, learn what helps, and — if and when you want it — bring in a mentor or professional for the next step.

How we hold your trust

Support that knows its limits.

MindCare AI is built to help you understand your emotions — not to replace the people trained to treat them.

We never diagnose. Assessments offer a starting signal, not a medical conclusion.

We're not a substitute for therapy. MindCare is a companion on the way to care, not a replacement for it.

Your journal is encrypted and private, by default, always — yours alone unless you choose to share it.

If things look serious, we say so — and point you toward real people and crisis resources, not just more app screens.
